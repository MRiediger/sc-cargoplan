# Ship silhouettes – source and license note

The polygons in `ship-outlines.json` are the top views of the ship models from cryon.rocks (Motherlode), extracted
from Star Citizen game files; CIG assets, non-commercial fan project. They were derived automatically (every triangle
filled into a raster, small gaps closed, the outer contour simplified) and keep the model's origin and size; length,
beam, height and floor come from the same models. The operators of Motherlode agreed to this use.

Three entries without `floorM` (135c, 600i Executive Edition, F7C Hornet Mk II) were traced from top-view renders
published on fleetyards.net (API `api.fleetyards.net/v1/models`), with their dimensions from the same API.
