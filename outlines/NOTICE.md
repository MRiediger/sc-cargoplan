# Ship silhouettes – source and license note

The polygons in `ship-outlines.json` are the top views of the ship models from cryon.rocks (Motherlode): cargo grid
data and ship models extracted from the Star Citizen game files; CIG assets, non-commercial fan project. They were
derived automatically (every triangle filled into a raster, small gaps closed, the outer contour simplified) and keep
the model's origin and size; length, beam, height and floor come from the same models. The operators of Motherlode
agreed to this use.

Three ships still use hull data from fleetyards.net: their entries without `floorM` (135c, 600i Executive Edition, F7C
Hornet Mk II) were traced from top-view renders published there (API `api.fleetyards.net/v1/models`), with their
dimensions from the same API.
