Load one side first: pick **Port first** or **Starboard first**, and CargoPlan fills every rack of that side before
the other, and on a wide deck its port or starboard half. The legend keeps the order you scanned in, a new scan closes
its destination's block, and Linux gets the command-line planner.

## Updating

- **From v0.6.0 or v0.5.x:** the overlay's bottom line says `Update available · v0.7.0`. Click **Update**; CargoPlan
  restarts with your plan, your ticks and the view as they were. If v0.6.0 came to you through that line, only the
  changes are downloaded; otherwise the full package (about 90 MB).
- **From v0.4.0 or older:** update by hand this once, as described in
  [Upgrading from v0.4.0 or older](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/releases/tag/v0.5.0) of v0.5.0, with
  `SC-CargoPlan-v0.7.0-win-x64.zip` below.

Check the download against the `.sha256` file
([how](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/wiki/Installation-and-first-start#check-the-download-optional)). Run
the game in **Borderless** window mode.

## Layout: one side first

Two new choices in the editor's layout line, **Sides** and **Side overflow**. Like the others, they take effect when
you press **Plan**. More:
[Plan and editor › Sides](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/wiki/Plan-and-editor#sides).

- **Sides:** **None** (as before), **Port first** or **Starboard first**.
  - **Racks by side:** on ships with racks left and right, like the Hull A, the Hull B, the Railen, the Carrack or the
    Hermes, every rack of the first side fills before a rack of the other; racks across the middle line come last.
    Contracts take the sides in the order of the legend.
  - **Halves of a rack:** a rack at least four cells wide, like the decks of the RAFT, the Perseus or the C2, splits its
    width into a port and a starboard half, and the first half fills before the other. Narrower racks, and racks that
    stack sideways like the Hull A's plates, keep their whole width.
- **Side overflow,** with a side and **Grouping: By destination:**
  - **Cross over:** a destination that doesn't fit the rest of the first side continues on the other, each part a
    block of its own.
  - **Wait:** every destination lies whole on one side, in the first that has room. One that fits neither side waits,
    even if single cells are free, and a line says so (`Rack 3: sides full – a mission waits (side overflow off)`,
    within a rack `Rack 1: lanes full – a mission waits (side overflow off)`).
  - **Wait** works when you press **Plan** or **Re-plan**. A contract you scan always comes in when there is room, if
    need be outside the blocks (`placed outside sections (capacity)`); the next **Plan** or **Re-plan** puts it on one
    side or lets it wait.
- **Blocks take whole rows:** on the RAFT, two contracts of 40 SCU take all 12 rows of the port side, and a third goes
  to starboard whole although single cells are still free.
- Moving a crate by hand ignores the sides, as it ignores blocks. With **Sides: None** every plan lies as in v0.6.0.

## Legend

- **Missions stand in the order you scanned them,** the latest at the bottom; missions you typed in the editor stand
  behind the scanned ones. Re-plan, ticks, undo and a restart keep the order. Before, they followed the order in which
  their destinations first appeared, so a third scan could stand above the second.
- The editor's **↑** and **↓** no longer move a scanned mission in the legend.

## Blocks per destination

With **Grouping: By destination**, a scan to a destination that already lies in a shared rack lays that destination's
block anew, with the new crates in it, instead of adding them beside it: the block stays closed, single crates lie next
to each other. Crates you ticked **loaded** or **delivered** stay where they are. If the block can't close around them,
the gap stays and a line says so (`Rack 1: Riker has a gap – loaded crates`).

## Linux: the command-line planner

Each release now also carries `SC-CargoPlan-Cli-v0.7.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` (about 66 MB, with its `.sha256`), for x64
with glibc and libicu. Unpack it with `tar -xzf` and run `./CargoPlan.Cli` in the folder `SC-CargoPlan-Cli`:
`plan <missions.json>`, `scan <screenshot.png>`, `ships --version`. There is no overlay on Linux
([why](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#d115--no-linux-overlay-port-the-cli-ships-for-linux-x64-f11-k12)),
and no updates: it plans with the ship data of its release, and newer ship data come with the next tarball.

## Files

- **`plan.json` is schema 11,** with `sides` and `sideOverflow`. v0.7.0 reads schema 8 to 10 as **None** and **Cross
  over** and saves the file as schema 11 right after loading; moves and ticks stay.
- **Mission files are schema 5.** Schema 3 and 4 files still import; without the new fields the editor keeps its
  choice.

Details: [plan-format.md](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/blob/main/docs/plan-format.md),
[mission-format.md](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/blob/main/docs/mission-format.md).

## Known issues

- **Going back to v0.6.0:** v0.6.0 can't read a schema 11 `plan.json`. It starts without a plan and says
  `Plan not loaded (…). The next save keeps the file aside, it is never overwritten.`; the first change keeps the file
  as `plan.json.unreadable-<date>-<time>`. Back on v0.7.0, close the overlay and rename that file to `plan.json`.
  Simpler: copy `%LocalAppData%\SC-CargoPlan\plan.json` before you go back, or start the old version with a data
  folder of its own (`SC-CargoPlan.exe --data-dir <full path>`). v0.6.0 refuses mission files of schema 5. The settings
  stay readable both ways.
- **A scan can move planned crates** of its destination in a shared rack (blocks per destination). Tick crates
  **loaded** once they are in the ship; then they stay.
- **Unsigned:** Windows warns the first time you start a downloaded ZIP: **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Small updates only one version apart:** the first update after unpacking a ZIP, or across more than one version,
  downloads the full package (about 90 MB).
- **A folder below `Program Files`:** the update asks Windows for administrator rights. Say No and the old version comes
  back with `Update failed · cancelled`; **Retry** asks again without downloading again.
- **Old ship data lose their holograms:** only the last two data releases keep their holograms on GitHub. **Update**
  the ship data to get them back.
- **135c and 600i Executive Edition** still use Fleetyards data: their racks lie in a generated row, not where they are
  in the ship.
- **Rows in the editor you haven't planned** are lost when you click **Update**, as when you close CargoPlan.

More, with workarounds: [Troubleshooting › Known issues](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/wiki/Troubleshooting#known-issues).
