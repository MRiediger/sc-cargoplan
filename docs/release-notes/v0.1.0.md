First release of SC-CargoPlan, a load-plan overlay for Star Citizen haulers: it decides which destination goes into which rack before you load, and shows it in a translucent window next to the game.

Unzip, then start `SC-CargoPlan\CargoPlan.App.exe`; Windows warns that the app is unsigned (**More info → Run anyway**). Check the download against the `.sha256` file. Run the game in **Borderless** window mode. How it works: the [wiki](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/wiki).

## Highlights

- One color per destination, one rack per destination where the ship allows it, every crate named in the legend.
- Contracts captured with ContractTracker come in by themselves; or type them in the editor.
- Drag, turn, split and merge crates to match what the cargo elevator really did.
- Mark each delivery loaded, then delivered at its stop; undo any step.
- Click-through while the game has the mouse. A separate window: nothing is injected into the game.
- 97 ships, with cargo grids from Fleetyards, plus the Aurora Mk II with its TS module.

## New since rc.3

rc.3 knew the MISC Hull A only. Since then:

- **Ships:** 97 ships to pick from the overlay's header, with a search and your recent ships.
- **Ship view:** one perspective 3D scene you turn, zoom and pan; each ship keeps its view after a restart. The ship's outline lies under the racks, or its 3D hologram stands around them (optional download, see below).
- **Plan:** the app no longer decides which stop comes first, the colors show what lies where. A crate dropped where it can't lie finds room, moving other crates if needed, instead of being refused. Re-plan, Discard plan, and Undo/Redo for every change to the plan, ticks included.
- **Crates:** turn a crate with the mouse wheel while you drag it; a selected crate tells where it comes from and where it goes.
- **At the elevator:** mark a delivery loaded with a click on its legend line; loaded cargo stays where it lies when you re-plan.
- **Look:** three presets (Default, High contrast, Minimal), export and import of a look, three color-blind-safe stop color sets, a legend that scrolls with many missions.
- **ContractTracker:** a contract that disappears from ContractTracker stays in the plan, marked gone (or leaves, if you choose so); a failed sync keeps your plan and writes `cargoplan.log`.

## Known issues

- **Unsigned, no auto-update:** Windows warns at the first start of every version; check the releases for new ones.
- **Ship data are drafts:** every grid except the MISC Hull A's comes from Fleetyards and isn't checked in the game; for some racks the data name no door limit, although the game may have one.
- **The Retaliator is missing:** there are no grid data for its cargo modules yet.
- **A crate under an empty rack** in front of it can't be clicked in the ship view. Turn the view, or use its line in the legend.
- **Very large ships zoomed in all the way** (such as the Hull C): the camera can stand among the racks, and parts close to it are cut off. Zoom out a little.
- **Hologram behind a half-transparent crate:** the parts of the hologram behind it don't show; hardly visible at the default fill.
- **Testers of the release candidates:** once 0.1.0 has saved your settings, an rc build can't read them. It starts with the default settings and says so; the file stays as it is until you change a setting there, and is then kept as `settings.json.bak`.
- **Ship holograms** are made from CIG's ship models and are not under MIT. They live in a release of their own, [`holo-v1`](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/releases/tag/holo-v1), with its own NOTICE, not in this ZIP. The app downloads them only from there, one file per ship when you pick a hologram look, and never fetches anything from Fleetyards; **Download ship holograms** in the settings switches it off.

More, with workarounds: [Troubleshooting › Known issues](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/wiki/Troubleshooting#known-issues).
