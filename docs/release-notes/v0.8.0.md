Plan a hauling run before you fly it. The new **Plan** tab shows the hauling contracts of Stanton and Pyro from the
game data, what your ship takes from a hub at once, and which loop pays most per hour. Pick a loop and **Start plan**:
the Cargo tab lists its contracts, you accept them at the terminal, F3 on each fills its line, and a station bar takes
you through the stops. The loading plan works as before.

## Updating

- **From v0.7.0, v0.6.0 or v0.5.x:** the overlay's bottom line says `Update available · v0.8.0`. Click **Update**;
  CargoPlan restarts with your plan, your ticks and the view as they were. If v0.7.0 came to you through that line, only
  the changes are downloaded; otherwise the full package (about 90 MB).
- **From v0.4.0 or older:** update by hand this once, as described in
  [Upgrading from v0.4.0 or older](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/releases/tag/v0.5.0) of v0.5.0, with
  `SC-CargoPlan-v0.8.0-win-x64.zip` below.

Check the download against the `.sha256` file
([how](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/wiki/Installation-and-first-start#check-the-download-optional)). Run
the game in **Borderless** window mode.

## New: the Plan tab

Under the header, **Cargo | Plan**. **Cargo** is the overlay as you know it; **Plan** is the planner. More:
[Plan tab](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/wiki/Plan-tab).

- **Matrix:** pickups in rows, destinations in columns; each cell counts the contracts between them and adds up their
  rewards (`16 · 1.8M`). Filters for system, area, rank, direct, contractor, faction and size; the tool tip lists the
  contracts with reward and SCU.
- **Recipe:** what to take from one hub at once with your ship: bulk, then direct, then small contracts, at most ten,
  each checked with the planner's own packing at the largest amount it can roll, plus the contracts to take back from
  the stations. With **Plan** selected, F3 on an offer says ` · fits as bulk` or ` · too big for MISC Hull B (…)`.
- **Loops:** a ring from each Stanton hub, direct runs there and back, the ring across the hubs from yours, and the
  rings of the Pyro stations, sorted by ~aUEC per hour, per loop or per landing. ~aUEC/h counts a time per landing, per
  crate and per long quantum jump (over 10 Gm), set in the settings.
- **Map:** the same contracts as lines between stations and planets, true to scale around the star. Click a station
  to see only what leaves there and comes back; wheel to zoom, drag to move, double-click to fit. Click a line to build
  a plan of your own from single contracts.

## New: a plan with its stops

- **Start plan** (in Recipe, Loops or Map) puts the plan into the Cargo tab: a grey block **Plan** above the legend,
  one line per contract to accept. Covalex contracts you had already accepted and scanned fill their lines.
- **At the terminal:** accept a contract of the plan and press F3 in the **ACCEPTED** tab; the scan fills its line
  (` · plan: bulk ✓ 1/10`) and its crates go into the ship as always. A contract the plan didn't list counts as an
  extra.
- **The station bar** above the block shows where you are: what to unload, what to accept there and whether it fits,
  the free contract slots. **‹ ›** move it; ticking the last delivery of a station moves it on.
- **Finish plan** at the hub keeps the round's aUEC and time; **End plan** drops the plan. Your cargo stays either way.
- **Measured times:** once you finished the same loop three times with the same ship, its row uses the median of your
  last seven rounds (`measured 3× · 1 h 20 min`). **Use my measured loop times (3+ runs)** in the settings switches it
  off.

## New: what your scans teach the planner

Every scan CargoPlan can read, in either tab, notes where a hauling contract really goes, how much it asked for and
what it pays. The game data name no destinations for the system-wide Covalex contracts and no reward for Red Wind's;
your scans fill that in. The matrix and the map mark what you have seen with `●`. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Settings

A section **Plan tab** in the settings window, before **Updates**: your rank and your faction (the boards show what
your rank may take, Red Wind offers each faction its own contracts), the three times behind ~aUEC/h, measured loop
times on or off, and **Map legend height** for the bar under the map.

## Changed

- **F3 on an offer while Plan is selected** stays out of your cargo:
  `Observed (Plan tab): Covalex Junior Small · 50,250 aUEC – cargo unchanged`. Accepted contracts go into the cargo in
  both tabs, and with **Cargo** selected F3 works as before. The overlay starts in **Cargo**.
- **The Plan tab takes the focus** while unlocked, so its scroll bars and the mouse wheel work over the game; click the
  game to give it back. The Cargo tab never takes it.
- **Words:** **Discard plan** is **Discard cargo** (button, hotkey and its line), the empty overlay says
  `No cargo. Open the editor to enter missions.`, and `Already in plan (#2)` is `Already in cargo (#2)`: "plan" now means
  the route. The hotkey keeps its key in `settings.json`.
- **Legend headers** name the destination of the mission's first line, short: `From Everus Harbor · to HUR-L5 · #1`.

## Files

- **New: `routes.json`** (schema 3) next to `plan.json` in `%LocalAppData%\SC-CargoPlan`: the observations from your
  scans, the plan you started, your finished rounds.
- **`settings.json`** gets a section `routes`; its schema stays 3.
- **`plan.json`** stays schema 11, **mission files** schema 5: nothing changes for saved plans and exported missions.

## Ship and hauling data

Built in: `2026.11.2`, 110 ships (one of them a cargo module configuration). If v0.7.0 has offered it to you already,
nothing changes for your ships. The same data release carries the hauling contract templates of Covalex and Red Wind
Linehaul and the positions of stations and planets, from game build 4.10.3, extracted with
[octfx/ScDataDumper](https://github.com/octfx/ScDataDumper) and published by the Star Citizen Wiki as
[StarCitizenWiki/scunpacked-data](https://github.com/StarCitizenWiki/scunpacked-data). They come and update with the
ship data, on the same **Update** click: no new network access, no game files in this repository.

## Known issues

- **Going back to v0.7.0:** v0.7.0 doesn't know the section `routes` and starts with default settings
  (`Settings not loaded, using defaults (The JSON property 'routes' could not be mapped …)`). To keep yours, close the
  overlay first, open `%LocalAppData%\SC-CargoPlan\settings.json` and delete the `"routes": { … }` block and the comma
  at the end of the line before it. Plan and mission files read in both versions.
- **Red Wind without reward:** Red Wind's templates carry no reward; a Red Wind loop shows `– aUEC` until you scan one
  of its contracts.
- **Red Wind's regional contracts** in Pyro are not shown yet.
- **Headhunters and CFP twins:** Red Wind offers the two factions contracts a scan can't tell apart; both share what
  you scanned. Pick your side with the faction filter.
- **The planner assumes every contract exists:** it plans with all templates of your board. Which ones the terminal
  offers right now, only the terminal shows.
- **Star Citizen as administrator:** run CargoPlan as administrator too; otherwise Windows hides key presses from it
  while the game is in front.
- **A scan can move planned crates** of its destination in a shared rack (blocks per destination). Tick crates
  **loaded** once they are in the ship; then they stay.
- **Unsigned:** Windows warns the first time you start a downloaded ZIP: **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Small updates only one version apart:** the first update after unpacking a ZIP, or across more than one version,
  downloads the full package (about 90 MB).
- **A folder below `Program Files`:** the update asks Windows for administrator rights. Say No and the old version comes
  back with `Update failed · cancelled`; **Retry** asks again without downloading again.
- **Old ship data lose their holograms:** only the last two data releases keep their holograms on GitHub. **Update**
  the ship data to get them back.
- **135c and 600i Executive Edition** still use Fleetyards data: their racks lie in a generated row, not where they are
  in the ship.
- **Rows in the editor you haven't planned** are lost when you click **Update**, as when you close CargoPlan.

More, with workarounds: [Troubleshooting › Known issues](https://github.com/MRiediger/sc-cargoplan/wiki/Troubleshooting#known-issues).
